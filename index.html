Anansi

Privacy policy

Anansi is run by [your business name]. It lets you connect your social media accounts, write a post once, and publish versions of it to the accounts you choose. This policy explains what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your Anansi account: your email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password. We never store the password itself.
  • Connected accounts: when you connect X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok, the platform gives us an access token and basic profile details (name, handle or page name, and account ID). Tokens are encrypted before they are stored.
  • What you post: the text, links and files you add, and a record of where each post went and whether it worked.
  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

How we use it

Only to do what you ask: show you your connected accounts, publish the posts you choose to the accounts you choose, and show you your history. We do not read your feeds, messages or followers, we do not post anything you didn't publish, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Who receives it

When you publish, the text and files for each version are sent to that platform: X, LinkedIn, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google (YouTube) or TikTok. Their own privacy policies apply from then on. Files you upload are kept at an unguessable web address so platforms such as Instagram and TikTok can download them.

YouTube

Anansi uses YouTube API Services to upload videos you choose to your channel. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Anansi's access at any time from your Google security settings.

How long we keep it

Until you remove it. Disconnecting an account deletes its token straight away. Deleting your Anansi account deletes everything: your login, every connected account, your post history and your uploaded files.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any account on the Accounts page.
  • Delete your whole account and all its data under Settings. See how to delete your data.
  • You can also revoke access from each platform's own settings.
  • For a copy of your data or any question about it, contact [your contact email].

Security

Passwords are hashed, platform tokens are encrypted, and the service should only be reached over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below changes too, and significant changes will be shown in the app.
