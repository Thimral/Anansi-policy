Deleting your data

You can delete your data from Anansi yourself, at any time.

Disconnect one account

  1. Log in and open Accounts.
  2. Press the remove button next to the account. Its access token is deleted immediately.

Delete everything

  1. Log in and open Settings.
  2. Under Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Anansi login, every connected account and its token, your post history, and every file you uploaded. Posts already published on X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok stay there; delete them on the platform itself.

Remove access from the platform side

You can also revoke Anansi's access in each platform's settings, for example Facebook's Business Integrations, or Google's security settings. If you can't log in, contact contact@thimral.com from the email address on your account and we will delete your data.
